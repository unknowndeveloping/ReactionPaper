For My Baby

A little letter from my heart to yours
One of my favorite memories with you ❤️
♡

I don't know if there's ever a perfect way to put everything I feel for you into words. Baka hindi sapat ang simpleng "I love you" para maipaliwanag kung gaano ka ka-halaga sa akin at kung gaano mo nabago ang buhay ko.

Before you, I never really understood how it feels to be loved in a way that makes you feel safe, cared for, and appreciated. Pero nung dumating ka, unti-unti kong naramdaman kung gaano kasarap sa pakiramdam ang magkaroon ng taong totoong nagmamalasakit at gustong-gusto akong makita masaya at okay. And that's exactly what you gave me.

You've always been so caring and sweet to me. Palagi mong sinisigurado na maayos ako, at hindi ka nagkukulang na iparamdam sa akin kung gaano mo ako kamahal.

But more than all that, I appreciate how you never got tired of loving me and understanding me, baby.

♡

Dahil din sa'yo, na-realize ko na ang pagmamahal pala ay hindi lang tungkol sa mga masasayang araw. Love is also about choosing to stay, lalo na kapag mahirap ang sitwasyon at kapag napakagulong ng paligid.

It's easy to stay when everything feels light and perfect, pero naniniwala ako na ang totoong pagmamahal ay nakikita kapag dumarating ang mga pagsubok.

Even on days when I'm not easy to understand, you still choose to be patient with me. You still stay. You still try.

At sobrang thankful ako roon, baby. Dahil ang totoong pagmamahal pala ay ang pagpili na manatili kahit wala tayong lahat ng sagot, at kahit nakakapagod o nakaka-overwhelm ang paligid.

It’s about choosing patience instead of walking away, choosing to understand, and reminding each other na hindi natin kailangang harapin ang lahat nang mag-isa.

♡

I hope you know that I notice all the little things you do:

♡ the way you take care of me

♡ yung pag-aalala mo na makauwi ako nang ligtas

♡ yung paglalaan mo ng oras para sa akin kahit busy ka

♡ yung pakikinig mo sa lahat ng sinasabi ko

♡ yung pag-unawa mo sa akin

♡ and the way you love me even on days when I don't feel like I deserve it.

I learned that love isn’t about promising that we’ll never have hard days. It’s about knowing na kapag dumating yung mga araw na yun, pipiliin pa rin natin ang isa’t isa at aayusin natin ang lahat together.

Kahit mahirap, kahit overwhelming, at kahit magulo yung sitwasyon, alam nating hindi natin kailangang harapin lahat nang mag-isa.

“We’ll still choose each other and face it together.”

And somehow, you did that for me.

Dahil ang pagmamahal ay hindi lang tungkol sa pagpili sa isa't isa kapag madali ang lahat. It’s also about choosing to understand, choosing to forgive, and choosing to stay even when things don’t go the way we expect.

Salamat din sa kung paano mo ako pinapatawa kahit sa mga napakasimpleng araw lang. Yung mga random jokes mo, yung paraan ng pagtingin mo sa akin, at kung paano mo ginagawang magaan ang lahat kapag stressed o pagod ako, sobrang mahalaga sa akin lahat ng 'yun.

You just have this natural way of making my world feel so much lighter and brighter, baby.

Gusto ko ring malaman mo na sobrang proud ako sa'yo, as in. Nakikita ko lahat ng effort at pagpapakahirap mo para sa mga pangarap mo at para sa ating dalawa.

Seeing how dedicated you are makes me respect you even more, at sobrang nae-excite ako sa future na unti-unti nating binubuo together.

♡

Kaya kahit ano man ang mangyari sa atin sa mga susunod na araw o taon, gusto ko sabay nating harapin ang lahat.

Through every high and low, I will always be right here cheering for you, supporting you, and loving you with all my heart.

Baka hindi ko man madalas masabi sa'yo, pero I really do appreciate you, baby. Higit pa sa kung gaano mo naiisip.

You deserve to know how much your love means to me. Sobrang nagpapasalamat ako na ikaw ang nakasama ko para maranasan ang ganitong klase ng pagmamahal, and if there’s one thing I hope you never doubt, it’s how grateful I am to have you, baby.

Sana nakikita mo rin ang sarili mo kung paano kita nakikita— napaka-caring, patient, loving, and always trying her best for the person she loves.

You’re such a good person, baby. Thank you for being my safe space, my peace, and my home.

❤️
